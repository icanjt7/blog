미 노동통계국가 2025-08-12 공개한 공식 발표를 바탕으로, 한국 독자가 확인할 핵심 맥락을 정리했습니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

In July, the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers rose 0.2 percent, seasonally adjusted, and rose 2.7 percent over the last 12 months, not seasonally adjusted.

## 핵심 내용

- The index for all items less food and energy increased 0.3 percent in July (SA); up 3.1 percent over the year (NSA).
- In July, the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers rose 0.2 percent, seasonally adjusted, and rose 2.7 percent over the last 12 months, not seasonally adjusted.
- The index for all items less food and energy increased 0.3 percent in July (SA); up 3.1 percent over the year (NSA).
- In July, the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers rose 0.2 percent, seasonally adjusted, and rose 2.7 percent over the last 12 months, not seasonally adjusted.
