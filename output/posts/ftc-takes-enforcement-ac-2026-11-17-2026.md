미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-11-17 공개한 'FTC Takes Enforcement Action Against Safety-Harness Manufacturer for Allegedly False Made in the USA Claims' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

The Federal Trade Commission is taking action against Elk River Inc., an Alabama-based manufacturer of safety harnesses and other fall protection equipment for workers, for falsely claiming that its harnesses are “Made in USA.” The proposed order settling the FTC’s allegations against Elk River imposes a $4.4 million judgment, which will be partially suspended, and prohibits the company from making misrepresentations that its products are of U.S. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- The FTC’s action against Elk River marks the fourth “Made in the USA” enforcement action taken by the Commission this year , advancing the FTC’s work to restore consumers’ confidence in American-made goods.
- In addition, the FTC issued warning letters in July to companies that appear to have misrepresented certain products as “Made in the USA.” “American workers using safety gear advertised as being ‘Made in the USA’ deserve the peace of mind knowing that those claims are 100% truthful,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “The FTC’s action today builds on the FTC’s commitment to ensuring American-made product claims are true and that businesses don’t falsely market products that fail to support American-based manufacturing.” According to a complaint filed by the FTC , Elk River made unqualified U.S.-origin claims, including that its safety harnesses are “Made in USA,” “American-made” and “#madeinalabama.” Elk River made these claims on its website, in print and online promotional materials, on social media, and on the harnesses’ labels, the FTC’s complaint alleges.
- Making an unqualified domestic origin claim requires the product to be “all or virtually all” made in the United States.
- While final assembly of the Elk River’s harnesses occurs in a manufacturing facility in Alabama, most components and materials—including components essential to the function and safe operation of the harnesses—originate outside the United States, according to the FTC’s complaint.
