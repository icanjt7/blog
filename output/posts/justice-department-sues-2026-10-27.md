## 어떤 발표인가

- The Department’s lawsuit enforces the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Protection of Pupil Rights Amendment (PPRA).
- “Schools should not discuss preferred pronouns or ‘gender identity’ with a student without parental notification and consent,” said Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K.
- Dhillon of the Justice Department’s Civil Rights Division.
- “Parents should not be lied to, stonewalled, or branded as ‘safety concerns’ when they ask to see the education records that reveal what schools are secretly doing to their children.
- This Department will end illegal school policies that keep parents in the dark.” The Department’s complaint alleges that AACPS’s gender policies deny parents their rights under FERPA and PPRA.
