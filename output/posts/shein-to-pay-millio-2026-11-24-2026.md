미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-11-24 공개한 'SHEIN to Pay $100 Million Over Allegations of Deceptive and Unfair Practices' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

SHEIN will pay $100 million in consumer redress to settle Federal Trade Commission allegations that the fast-fashion retailer made it unreasonably difficult for consumers to resolve purchase issues, failed to keep its shipping promises, suppressed negative reviews and made deceptive supply chain and cotton-origin claims, all in violation of the law. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- “SHEIN attracted consumers with promises of affordable clothing, fast delivery and use of suppliers that earned above-average ratings on standards related to child and forced labor, among other representations,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “In reality, the company misled millions of consumers about its products, its suppliers and the authenticity of its customer reviews.
- SHEIN further compounded consumer harm by making it extraordinarily difficult to obtain customer service.” In a complaint , the FTC alleges that, through its practices, SHEIN violated Section 5 of the FTC Act, the Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, and the Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials.
- The complaint alleges that SHEIN marketed fast, transparent and reliable shipping but tens of thousands of consumers reported that their orders arrived days, weeks or months late, while some consumers never received their orders at all.
- SHEIN did not provide consumers with adequate shipping updates, including legally required delay notices, and consumers were unable to reach customer service to inquire about late or missing orders.
