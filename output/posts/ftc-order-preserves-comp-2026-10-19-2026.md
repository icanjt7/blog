미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-10-19 공개한 'FTC Order Preserves Competition in Digital Advertising Ecosystem as Part of Publicis’s Acquisition of LiveRamp' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

The Federal Trade Commission has taken steps to preserve competition in the digital advertising ecosystem by requiring Publicis Groupe S.A. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- to continue providing neutral and nondiscriminatory access to LiveRamp Holdings Inc.’s products and services, and to establish firewalls to prevent Publicis from accessing LiveRamp’s customer data, in order to complete Publicis’s acquisition of the company.
- The proposed consent order would address the FTC’s concerns that Publicis’s acquisition of LiveRamp, which provides data connectivity services that help facilitate the secure and confidential movement of data used for digital advertising, would harm competition in the markets for media agency services and audience data services.
- Under the $2.2 billion deal, Publicis, which provides media agency services including media planning and buying for digital advertising campaigns, would acquire 100% of LiveRamp.
- The deal would likely result in Publicis having the ability and incentive to foreclose customers of competing media agency service providers by using its control of LiveRamp to push customers to switch their media agency business to Publicis even if those customers otherwise prefer competing media agencies, according to the FTC’s complaint .
- “By requiring LiveRamp to continue operating as it does today as a neutral platform, this order will ensure that Publicis does not gain an unfair advantage over its competitors in the digital advertising marketplace,” said David Shaw, Principal Deputy Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Competition.
