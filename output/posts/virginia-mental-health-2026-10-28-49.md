## 어떤 발표인가

- 20 against Mikia Antoneice Noble, 37, of North Chesterfield, Virginia, for a $49 million mental health crisis services fraud scheme targeting Virginia Medicaid.
- Noble was arrested today.
- According to court documents, Noble was the co-owner and Chief Operating Officer of Advancing Communities Everywhere LLC (ACE), a mental health provider in the Richmond, Virginia area.
- In addition to her ownership role, Noble claimed to also work as a mental health professional for ACE.
- ACE purported to provide mental health services to individuals experiencing acute or significant behavioral health crises but did not provide those services as billed.
