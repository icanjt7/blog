Nicholas Kent was sworn in as the 15th Under Secretary of Education on August 4, 2025, following his nomination by President Donald J.

## 핵심 내용

- As of February 5, 2026, Kent has also been delegated the duties of Deputy Secretary of Education.
- As a first-generation college student and a respected education policy expert with more than two decades of experience, Kent serves as the nation’s top federal official for higher education.
- As Under Secretary, Kent oversees the Department’s postsecondary education, career, technical, and adult education, and federal student aid programs.
- This includes managing the $1.6 trillion federal student loan portfolio and the over $30 billion Pell Grant program.
