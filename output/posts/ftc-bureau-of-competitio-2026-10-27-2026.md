## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

## 어떤 발표인가

- State and municipal governments spend hundreds of millions of dollars on playground equipment every year, so taxpayers are among the biggest beneficiaries from continued head-to-head competition between PlayPower and Burke.
- Tens of millions of children and their families will also benefit from higher quality, better service and greater playground innovation.
- The top five manufacturers—including PlayPower and Burke—control 85%–90% of the market for commercial outdoor playground equipment.
- This proposed merger is the latest in a series of acquisitions by PlayPower.
- It would have cut the number of major competitors down from five to four and would have given PlayPower’s owners control over more than 30% of the relevant market.
