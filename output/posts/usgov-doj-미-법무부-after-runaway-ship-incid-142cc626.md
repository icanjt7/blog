## 발표에서 확인되는 내용

- MSC Shipmanagement Limited (MSC), a vessel operating company, was sentenced to pay a criminal fine of $6 million and to serve a four-year term of probation for failing to report a hazardous condition on board the containership MSC Michigan VII and to obstructing a National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) and U.S.
- Coast Guard (USCG) investigation .
- MSC must also conduct a root-cause analysis of the circumstances relating to the hazardous conditions onboard the MSC Michigan VII .
- MSC’s chief engineer, Fernando San Diego San Juan, was sentenced to pay a $2,000 fine.
- He previously pleaded guilty, also for failing to report a hazardous condition and obstructing the investigation.
