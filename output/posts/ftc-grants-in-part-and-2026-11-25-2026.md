## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

The Federal Trade Commission today modified a 2018 order related to Northrop Grumman Corporation’s acquisition of aerospace and defense contractor Orbital ATK, narrowing the order’s scope to apply only to missile competitions involving large solid rocket motors (SRMs). 우주·과학 분야 발표는 국내 연구기관, 대학, 항공우주 산업 종사자가 기술 협력 흐름을 살필 때 참고할 만합니다. 임무 일정, 관측 장비, 연구 목표처럼 원문에 적힌 고유 정보를 중심으로 보는 것이 좋습니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- In 2018, the Commission issued a final consent order to resolve allegations that Northrop’s acquisition of Orbital would harm competition.
- The order requires Northrop to supply SRMs to rival missile suppliers on a nondiscriminatory basis and to maintain a firewall separating its SRM business from the rest of the company’s operations.
- SRMs are an essential input for missile systems, providing the propulsion necessary to carry missiles to their intended targets.
- Earlier this year, Northrop petitioned the Commission to set aside the 2018 order in its entirety.
- In evaluating the petition, the FTC worked closely with the Department of War to assess current and future military requirements for SRMs and the competitive conditions affecting their procurement.
