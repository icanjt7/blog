미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-10-13 공개한 'FTC Sues Operator of Sextortion Relief Scheme for Misleading Consumers into Believing it Would Identify Criminals and End Sextortion Attacks' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

A federal court has granted the Federal Trade Commission’s request to temporarily halt the activities of Digital Forensics Corporation LLC and its CEO for allegedly defrauding consumers out of millions of dollars using false and deceptive promises relating to “sextortion assistance services.” Sextortion is a rapidly growing threat to Americans, routinely carried out by foreign scammers who threaten to publish a nude or sexual image of the victim unless the victim pays the criminal. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- In a complaint the FTC alleges that Digital Forensics , along with its owner Dmitry Belkin and several affiliated companies, misled victims of sextortion into paying at least $60 million by falsely claiming that it would provide sextortion assistance services.
- “Digital Forensics preys on consumers already in crisis, exploiting victims of sextortion by using fear to pressure them into paying thousands of dollars for services that are never provided,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “The FTC is committed to protecting consumers from sextortion and other crimes involving non-consensual intimate visual images.
- That includes taking action against companies that take advantage of consumers experiencing a crisis and engage in deceptive practices that prey on victims’ vulnerability and use abusive tactics that exacerbate the harm.” The FTC alleged that Digital Forensics pushed victims to purchase a “Phase 1” package of sextortion assistance services, which was supposed to include tracking the sextortionist’s location, communicating with the sextortionist, producing a detailed investigative report and reporting the crime to law enforcement.
- Despite charging consumers between $1,499 and $20,000, as alleged in the complaint, Digital Forensics, in numerous instances, did not perform the promised services.
