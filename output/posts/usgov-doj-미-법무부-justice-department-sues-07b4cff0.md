## 발표에서 확인되는 내용

- The complaint alleges that the County’s newly enacted Bill 23-26 violates the Second Amendment by prohibiting law-abiding citizens from possessing firearms in daily life.
- Bill 23-26 prohibits carrying firearms at thousands of locations, and also creates an arbitrary 100-yard exclusion zone around each location.
- The law applies even if the person carrying a firearm is duly licensed and has the express consent of the property owner.
- “The Second Amendment does not allow local governments to ban law-abiding citizens from carrying firearms in public places as they go about their daily lives,” said Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K.
- Dhillon of the Justice Department’s Civil Rights Division.
