미 노동통계국가 2025-08-01 공개한 공식 발표를 바탕으로, 한국 독자가 확인할 핵심 맥락을 정리했습니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

Total nonfarm payroll employment changed little in July (+73,000) and has shown little change since April.

## 핵심 내용

- The unemployment rate, at 4.2 percent, changed little in July.
- Employment continued to trend up in health care and in social assistance.
- Federal government continued to lose jobs.
- Total nonfarm payroll employment changed little in July (+73,000) and has shown little change since April.
