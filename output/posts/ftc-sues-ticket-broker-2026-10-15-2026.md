미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-10-15 공개한 'FTC Sues Ticket Broker Over Unlawful Tactics Used to Buy Millions of Tickets to Popular Live Events' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

The Federal Trade Commission sued REPS & Company Inc., alleging the ticket broker illegally circumvents measures designed to limit the number of tickets that can be purchased for a single event, purchasing millions of tickets to high-demand events that it later resells at a significant profit. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- The complaint , filed by the Department of Justice upon notification and referral from the FTC, alleges REPS, along with its CEO Ryan Pancheri, President Brian Archibald and Chief Operating Officer Nicholas Totagrande, violated the Better Online Ticket Sales Act.
- The law makes it illegal to “circumvent a security measure, access control system or other technological control or measure on an Internet website or online service that is used by the ticket issuer to enforce posted event ticket limits or to maintain the integrity of posted online ticket purchasing order rules.” The complaint alleges that, since 2022, REPS and its operators used a variety of unlawful tactics to bypass security measures from ticket issuers in order to exceed ticket limits set by ticket issuers.
- REPS purchased more than eight tickets for at least 83,500 events—many of which were high demand and likely to sell out.
- REPS spent more than $1.3 billion to amass more than 18.5 million tickets for these events, generating at least $120.8 million in profit by reselling these tickets on the secondary marketplace.
- For example, REPS generated $833,000 in profit by using 1,300 accounts to purchase 7,624 tickets to just one Beyonce Renaissance tour concert in Kansas City, Mo., in 2023, according to the complaint.
