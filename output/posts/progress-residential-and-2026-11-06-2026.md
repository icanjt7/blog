## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

The nation’s largest property manager of single-family rental homes, Progress Residential, and its owner, private equity firm Pretium Partners, will pay $49.75 million to settle charges brought by the Federal Trade Commission and four states that the companies misled consumers with deceptive rental pricing practices. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- This is the largest enforcement matter taken against a landlord in the Commission’s history.
- The FTC and Arizona, Colorado, Indiana and Utah allege Progress and Pretium advertised false rental prices, charged hidden fees, failed to inspect homes before residents moved in, improperly deducted amounts from renters’ security deposits, restricted negative reviews, and failed to provide adequate customer service.
- The complaint alleges that, since at least 2021, these hidden fees, which often go undiscovered until after consumers have signed a lease or moved in, have cost consumers tens of millions of dollars.
- “Consumers searching for housing should have access to straightforward information about the costs associated with renting a home,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “Transparency regarding rental prices and fees helps consumers compare options and make informed choices.
