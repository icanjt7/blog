## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

## 어떤 발표인가

- The report, Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026 , details the FTC’s efforts to stop unfair or deceptive acts or practices that impact older Americans through law enforcement actions, consumer education campaigns and outreach, and coordination with partners.
- It also highlights data analysis of consumer reports on fraud targeting older adults, including estimates that the overall cost of fraud to older adults in 2025 could be as high as $119.5 billion, “The FTC is committed to protecting older Americans from scams, including by taking every action necessary to shut down fraud operations,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “This latest report showcases our enforcement actions, outreach efforts and other initiatives the FTC has undertaken to defend older adults from fraudulent enterprises and educate Americans on how best to protect themselves from scams.” Older Adult Fraud Reporting Findings The report outlines key findings from reports submitted in 2025 to the Consumer Sentinel Network, which collects reports from consumers about fraud and other consumer problems.
- Key findings include: Total fraud losses reported by older adults (ages 60 and over) increased about fivefold from 2020 to 2025, skyrocketing from about $600 million in 2020 to approximately $3 billion in 2025.
- This increase was driven largely by growing reports of extraordinarily large individual losses.
