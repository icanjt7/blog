## 발표에서 확인되는 내용

- Trump Restores American Commercial Fishing in the Pacific The White House June 11, 2026 UNLEASHING ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY IN THE PACIFIC: Today, President Donald J.
- Trump signed a proclamation to restore commercial fishing access to nearly half a million square miles in the Pacific, advancing the America First Fishing Policy.
- The proclamation restores commercial fishing access to the Mau and Ho‘omalu Zones of the Papahānaumokuākea Marine National Monument, the Islands Unit of the Mariana Trench Marine National Monument, and the Rose Atoll Marine National Monument.
- PUTTING AMERICA FIRST IN SEAFOOD COMPETITIVENESS: President Trump is committed to removing unnecessary restrictions on American fishermen in order to enhance U.S.
- domestic seafood production and lower seafood prices, support American jobs, and promote food and national security.
