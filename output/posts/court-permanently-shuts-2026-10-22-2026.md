## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

At the Federal Trade Commission’s request, a federal court has permanently banned the operators of an alleged debt relief scheme that targeted seniors and veterans by impersonating banks, credit card companies and government agencies. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- On October 14, 2026, the court entered a default judgment of $106.8 million against corporate defendants Accelerated Debt Settlement Inc.
- , ADS Resolve LLC, Financial Solutions Group LLC, Unified Capital Services LLC, Mediwerks, Resolution Specialists LLC and Futura Capital banning the corporate and individual defendants involved from the debt relief industry.
- “Targeting seniors and veterans, pretending to be entities they know and trust, and then robbing them of their hard-earned money is egregious behavior,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “The FTC will continue to take action against impersonation and illegal debt relief schemes.” In July 2025, at the FTC’s request, a federal court temporarily halted the Accelerated Debt Settlement operation.
- The complaint alleged the defendants falsely claimed to be banks and government entities and that they could reduce consumers’ debt by as much as 75% or more.
