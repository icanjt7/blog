## 어떤 발표인가

- Inc., by prohibiting anticompetitive entanglements between the two companies.
- The FTC alleged that Beretta and Ruger’s proposed stock purchase deal would create an illegal interlocking directorate arrangement in violation of Section 8 of the Clayton Act.
- The statute generally prohibits directors and officers from serving simultaneously on the boards of competitors.
- The final consent order prohibits Beretta, a subsidiary of Upifra S.A., from appointing or nominating anyone to serve on Ruger’s board of directors unless that person is independent of Beretta.
- The order also requires Beretta to provide the Commission with at least 15 days’ written notice before appointing, designating, nominating, electing or otherwise causing any person to become a member of the board of directors of Ruger.
