## 어떤 발표인가

- The following is according to the indictment: from 2019 to the present, Gustavo Rojano Salazar, Gabriel Rojano Salazar and Andres Huerta Rendon, who all resided in the Atlanta area and were Mexican citizens, obtained Mexican identification documents in the names of third parties.
- They used these identification documents to obtain Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers (ITIN) with the IRS.
- An ITIN was a number issued by the IRS to an individual who was required to file a federal tax return, but who did not have or was not eligible to receive a Social Security number.
- The three co-conspirators directed the IRS to mail the ITIN letters to post office boxes they controlled.
- In total, the co-conspirators obtained a significant number of Mexican Identification cards and ITIN letters, as depicted in the photo below: Mexican Identification cards and ITIN letters After receiving the ITIN information, the co-conspirators prepared and filed fraudulent tax returns in the names of the Mexican ITIN holders that included fabricated Forms W-2 reporting certain income and withholdings.
