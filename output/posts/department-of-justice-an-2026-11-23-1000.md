## 어떤 발표인가

- CHP is a competitive grant program that provides funding directly to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement agencies to pay for the entry-level salaries and fringe benefits of newly hired and rehired full-time sworn officers/deputies to make America safe again.
- CHP is a flagship program in the COPS Office, which has a long history of promoting police partnerships.
- Through CHP, this Administration supports our nation’s front-line heroes by providing them with needed resources and expertise to enhance policing strategies and tactics.
- The express purpose of CHP is crime reduction through effective common-sense policing.
