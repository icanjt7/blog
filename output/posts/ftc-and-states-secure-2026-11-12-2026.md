미 연방거래위원회가(Federal Trade Commission) 2026-11-12 공개한 'FTC and 17 States Secure $63 Million Settlement with Family Dollar Stores in Price Transparency Win for Consumers' 발표를 바탕으로, 확인 가능한 사실을 중심으로 정리했습니다.

## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

Family Dollar will pay $63 million to settle Federal Trade Commission and state allegations that the company routinely charged customers more at the register than the prices displayed on shelves, price tags and advertisements, costing consumers millions of dollars. 소비자 보호나 개인정보, 결제 관행과 관련된 발표라면 국내 서비스 이용자도 유사한 약관, 환불, 데이터 활용 방식을 비교해 볼 수 있습니다. 특히 플랫폼 기업이나 해외 서비스를 쓰는 경우에는 미국 규제기관이 문제 삼은 행위가 무엇인지 확인하는 것이 도움이 됩니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- The proposed order prohibits Family Dollar from misrepresenting the price of its goods or services and requires the retailer to establish and implement a pricing accuracy program that is independently assessed and annually certified.
- “The FTC is committed to ensuring price transparency and fairness so consumers can make informed choices,” said Christopher Mufarrige, Director of the FTC’s Bureau of Consumer Protection.
- “Today’s order will ensure Family Dollar stops deceiving consumers about its prices and returns money to consumers harmed by these practices.” According to the complaint , thousands of Family Dollar stores nationwide have overcharged shoppers, including in low-income communities with limited access to full-service grocery stores, while failing to correct the issue despite being warned about the problem.
- The complaint also alleges that consumers from across the country have consistently reported widespread pricing discrepancies, and regulatory inspections have confirmed that price labels are inaccurate in stores across the nation, with Family Dollar frequently charging higher prices at the register.
- In the last five years, state and local regulators have cited Family Dollar for thousands of pricing violations, resulting in fines exceeding $3 million.
