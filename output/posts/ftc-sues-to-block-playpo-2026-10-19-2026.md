## 핵심 내용과 국내 파급력

Today, the Federal Trade Commission sued to block Platinum Equity and its portfolio company PlayPower’s acquisition of BCI Burke, alleging the deal to combine two of the largest commercial outdoor playground equipment makers will drive up prices and decrease innovation and quality for equipment bought by schools, churches, parks and recreation departments and other organizations across the country. 우주·과학 분야 발표는 국내 연구기관, 대학, 항공우주 산업 종사자가 기술 협력 흐름을 살필 때 참고할 만합니다. 임무 일정, 관측 장비, 연구 목표처럼 원문에 적힌 고유 정보를 중심으로 보는 것이 좋습니다.

## 어떤 발표인가

- PlayPower, owned by private equity firm Platinum Equity, proposes to buy Burke, one of PlayPower’s closest competitors in the manufacture of commercial outdoor playground equipment.
- The proposed transaction would add the Burke brand to PlayPower’s existing commercial outdoor playground portfolio, which includes Miracle Recreation Equipment Company, Little Tikes Commercial and Playworld Systems, Inc.—brands PlayPower previously acquired.
- The complaint alleges that the deal will significantly increase concentration in an already concentrated market and eliminate PlayPower and Burke’s intense rivalry that benefits playground customers through lower prices, better services, higher quality and increased innovation.
- The complaint is joined by the attorneys general of Arizona, California, Colorado, Illinois, Nevada, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wisconsin.
- “Playgrounds are an indispensable part of daily life for millions of American kids and their families.
